pcapsummary turns raw network capture exports into a readable summary in seconds. You point it at a text file that a tool like tshark exported from a packet capture, and it tells you which devices were talking most, what protocols were in use, and which network flows carried the most traffic. It is a command-line tool for network analysts, security teams, and developers who need a quick, scriptable way to make sense of large packet captures without manually digging through thousands of rows.
